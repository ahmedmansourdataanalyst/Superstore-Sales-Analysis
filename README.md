# Superstore Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Superstore sales data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, and business trends using Excel and Power BI.

## Objectives
- Analyze total sales and profit.
- Compare performance across product categories and regions.
- Identify sales and profitability trends.
- Present key performance indicators through interactive dashboards.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Cleaning and Transformation
- Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: 2,297,201
- Total Profit: 286,397

## Key Insights
- Generated approximately **$2.30M in total sales** and **$286K in total profit**, achieving an overall profit margin of approximately **12.5%**.
- Identified products with strong sales performance but relatively low or negative profitability, highlighting opportunities for better pricing and discount strategies.
- Observed differences in sales and profitability contributions across the Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.
- Identified variations in sales and profitability across regions and states, highlighting differences in regional business performance.
